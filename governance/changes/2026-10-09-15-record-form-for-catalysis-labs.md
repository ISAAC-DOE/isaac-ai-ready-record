# 2026-10-09-15 The portal's record form can enter a catalysis lab's result

- **Product:** repository (the portal's manual entry form). **Kind:** portal interface. **Owner:** D. Sokaras (PI).
- **Why now:** the PI's direction of 2026-10-09: catalysis first for the science, any data for the repository;
  every catalysis lab should be able to enter its own performance results.

## Problem

A typical lab entry made from the form's fields (a CO2 reduction run with its reaction, cell type and potential
scale) was rejected:
- no field for the control mode, the potential, the current density, the electrolyte or the pH, so the most
  important conditions of an electrocatalysis result could only be typed as JSON;
- one result value per record, so a product distribution could not be entered;
- "generated by" and an uncertainty required by the schema, neither explained, and a blank uncertainty left the
  value invalid;
- a free-text value source and link basis, where the schema takes a fixed list;
- a checksum of 64 zeros written on every literature source (the pattern change 09 now warns about);
- warnings never shown: the form showed errors and holds only, so a saved record's warnings appeared only later
  on the Saved Records page.

## Change

- A rules box at the top: one result per record, conditions as measured or stated, links only for a stated
  relation, the producer named; links to the tutorial, the contract page and the Links page.
- Conditions: temperature basis; control mode, potential (stored as given on its scale, projected to RHE only when
  given vs RHE, never converted), current density, reference electrode, electrolyte and concentration, pH and its
  basis; feed phase, composition, flow rate and pressure for thermal, photo- and flow catalysis.
- Results: a table, one row per value; a blank uncertainty is written as not reported; values entered by hand are
  `manual`; "who or what produced these values" explained.
- The link basis from its list; the asset checksum explained (blank writes `not_available`); the literature source
  takes `not_available_literature_source` and its citation relation.
- Warnings and notes shown at preview and at save.

## Gate 1

No check changes. Tests: the form's builder turns a CO2 reduction entry, a potential on another scale, a
galvanostatic run, a thermal CO oxidation entry and a CuO reference spectrum into records the validator publishes;
the real form, driven headlessly as a person would, produces the CO2 reduction record with its conditions and
values (`tests/test_record_form.py`, `tests/test_record_form_ui.py`; CI now installs Streamlit to run the second).
Suite: 951 passed.

## Gate 2: review

Codex, Grok and Gemini, blind, on the change and its texts. All three: a lab member would not yet fill it in
correctly. Their points, all taken:
- **Bugs:** a galvanostatic run's measured potential on a scale other than RHE was dropped (now a result,
  `steady_state_potential` in `V_ref` or `V_SHE`, the scale named in its definition); a current typed for a
  potentiostatic run became an applied setpoint (now the field is "galvanostatic runs only" and such an entry is
  refused with its fix); "±0.008" was read as no uncertainty (± and +/- are now read; text is refused); a blank
  feed phase became gas (now asked for).
- **Misreadings:** temperature typed in °C into a kelvin box (a °C or K choice, °C converted exactly); Faradaic
  efficiencies typed as 42.8 (the results help says fraction 0.428 or unit percent); "who produced these values"
  confused with the producing group (now "Analysis or software that produced these values, optional"); one
  electrolyte for a divided cell (now the working-electrode electrolyte and the anolyte).
- **Rules box:** "One record holds one result" contradicted the results table; now one sample, technique and
  condition set per record, each value a row. The warnings label no longer says "accepted" when a hold warning
  keeps the record private.
- **Missing fields, ranked by all three:** iR compensation (added: method, percent, whether the reported potential
  is corrected), electrode area and catalyst loading (added, with electrode type), product analysis method and the
  light source for photocatalysis (not added: the first needs a design for several products, the second a schema
  field; both listed in PROPOSED.md).

Tests after review: 20 form tests (12 on the builder, 2 driving the real form), suite 956 passed.
